# Try without logging in

Browse a server's public content with no sign-up and no email address. Use it when you want to get a feel for the app first, or to look around other servers without logging in.

## Add a guest

In the login window, enter the server's host name and then choose "**Browse as guest**" (ゲストとして閲覧). No authentication happens; it is added as an account that only uses the server's public API.

You can have guests on several servers at once, and mix them with logged-in accounts.

## What you can and cannot do

::: tip You can

- Read public timelines (local, global and more)
- Browse public channels, gallery posts and pages
- Look at server info, custom emoji and federation charts
- Use local tools such as themes and widgets

:::

::: warning You cannot

- Post, react or follow
- Receive notifications
- Manage drive, lists, antennas or clips

:::

Columns that are not available show a note in the "Add column" (カラム追加) list and cannot be selected.

What you can see depends on each server's visibility settings. On servers that show nothing to logged-out users, a guest sees nothing.

## To stop

Just remove the guest account. Choose "**Remove guest**" (ゲストを削除) from the account menu. Nothing is left on the server.

If you want to log in for real, [add an account](/en/docs/first-run#_1-add-an-account) as usual. Guests and logged-in accounts are separate, so there is no need to remove the guest first.
